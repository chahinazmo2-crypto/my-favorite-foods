Pizza Recipe 🍕

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1 teaspoon of yeast
  • 1 teaspoon of sugar
  • 3/4 cup of warm water
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olives and oregano

Preparation:

1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and warm water.
2. Add olive oil and knead the dough.
3. Let the dough rest for 1 hour.
4. Spread the dough and add tomato sauce.
5. Add cheese, olives and oregano.
6. Bake at 200°C for 15-20 minutes.

Chicken Shawarma Recipe 🌯

Ingredients:

  • 300 g of chicken breast
  • 2 tablespoons of yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon of paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon of cumin
  • Salt and pepper
  • Pita bread and garlic sauce

Preparation:

1. Cut the chicken into thin strips.
2. Mix yogurt, lemon juice, garlic and spices.
3. Add chicken and marinate for 30 minutes.
4. Cook the chicken in a pan until golden.
5. Put chicken in pita bread.
6. Add garlic sauce and vegetables, then serve.

Potato Bechamel Recipe 🥔

Ingredients:

  • 4 potatoes
  • 2 tablespoons of butter
  • 2 tablespoons of flour
  • 2 cups of milk
  • 1 cup of grated cheese
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation:

1. Peel and slice the potatoes.
2. Boil them until slightly tender.
3. Melt butter in a pan and add flour.
4. Gradually add milk while stirring.
5. Add salt and pepper to the sauce.
6. Place potatoes in a baking dish.
7. Pour the béchamel sauce over them.
8. Add cheese and bake at 180°C for 20 minutes.

Double Cheeseburger Recipe 🍔

Ingredients:

  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 beef patties (about 100 g each)
  • 2 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 1 tomato
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 2 tablespoons of mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation:

1. Season the beef patties with salt and pepper.
2. Cook them in a hot pan until fully cooked.
3. Add cheddar cheese on top.
4. Toast the burger buns.
5. Spread mayonnaise on the buns.
6. Add lettuce, tomato and the two patties.
7. Close the burger and serve.

Crispy Potato Balls Recipe 🥔

Ingredients:

  • 3 large potatoes
  • 1/2 cup of grated cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup of breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons of flour
  • Salt and pepper
  • Oil for frying

Preparation:

1. Boil the potatoes until soft.
2. Mash them in a bowl.
3. Add cheese, salt and pepper.
4. Shape the mixture into small balls.
5. Dip each ball in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
6. Fry until golden and crispy.
7. Serve warm.

French Fries Recipe 🍟

Ingredients:

  • 4 large potatoes
  • Oil for frying
  • Salt

Preparation:

1. Peel and wash the potatoes.
2. Cut them into thin strips.
3. Soak them in cold water for 20 minutes.
4. Drain and dry them well.
5. Heat oil in a frying pan.
6. Fry the potatoes until golden and crispy.
7. Add salt and serve hot.
